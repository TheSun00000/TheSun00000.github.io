Nazim Bendib

I'm a PhD student in AI at Sorbonne University (ISIR, MLIA team), advised by Olivier Sigaud and Nicolas Perrin-Gilbert. I work on teaching agents to solve tasks they have never seen: zero-shot reinforcement learning and Behavioral Foundation Models. Before that, I worked on RL for compilers at NYU Abu Dhabi and on multi-agent RL at InstaDeep.

Outside research, I play the piano and video games, and I used to organize AI hackathons in Algeria.
